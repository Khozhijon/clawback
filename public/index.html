Clawback

Find the money that leaked. Then ask for it back.

Drop in a bank statement. Clawback finds double charges, price rises, forgotten trials and bank fees, and an AI model on your own device writes the refund and cancellation messages for you.

Drop a statement here or choose a file

CSV from any bank. The file is read by your browser.

No statement to hand?

  1. 1

    It finds the leaks

    Charges on a rhythm, prices that crept up, the same bill taken twice, a $1 trial that became $22.99 a month, fees your bank hoped you would not add up. Plain arithmetic, shown with the lines behind it.

  2. 2

    It writes the message

    For each leak, a refund or cancellation message drafted by Gemini Nano inside Chrome. Your statement lines are used on this device and go nowhere.

  3. 3

    You hold the switch

    If your device has no built-in model, the cloud can step in, but only after you have seen the exact text that would be sent and said yes. Every request is listed in a ledger.

How the AI is used. Every generative request goes through Firebase AI Logic. On-device requests use Gemini Nano in Chrome; cloud requests use Gemini through the same SDK and happen only with your say. Sums, dates and alerts are computed by ordinary code, never by the model.

Clawback does not give financial advice. It adds up what your statement already says and helps you write to the people who took the money.

Where the AI runs

A one-time download by Chrome (several GB). After that, drafting works with no network.
